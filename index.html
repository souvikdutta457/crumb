Good to know

⚠️ Allergy information
Our brownies may contain or come into contact with common allergens such as eggs, wheat, dairy and nuts. If you have an allergy, please message us before ordering.

Storage: keep in a cool, dry place and enjoy within 2 days.

How to order

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your box choice, your tower and flat number.
  2. Your order joins the current batch.
  3. We confirm your order and a delivery time.
  4. Pay by UPI. We deliver once your payment is received.

Delivery

Currently delivering within Prestige Shantiniketan, Whitefield. Please mention your tower and flat number when you order. More areas coming soon.

How our batches work

Fresh, limited batches. We don’t bake until a batch is full, so nothing sits around. Once your order is paid and the batch fills up, we bake it and bring it to your door straight after. If a batch is already full, you’re first in line for the next one.

Our story

Hi, I’m Souvik, a college student who has always loved eating brownies. Baking them, though, turned out to be a whole different adventure, and I fell in love with it.

What began as sharing my brownies with everyone around me has become Crumb: small batches, real chocolate cravings, and a little piece of joy in every box. We’re starting with our Classic Fudgy, and there’s much more to come.

Thank you for being part of the journey. — Souvik
